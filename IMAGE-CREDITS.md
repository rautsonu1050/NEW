# YATRA AI destination photo assets

13 downloaded and visually checked real photos; 11 destination cards plus two landmark alternates. Every final file is 1600 px wide and landscape. Existing portrait sources were cropped for landscape cards; no generated art or retouching was used.

Each record in asset-manifest.json provides the exact local file, image URL, source page/seed-file provenance, known photographer, dimensions, crop, and licensing reference.

Photos sourced from Unsplash: https://unsplash.com/license . That license permits free commercial and non-commercial use without required attribution; attribution is appreciated. Free photo pages explicitly identify the Unsplash License for searched assets. Photographer and individual photo pages for supplied seed URLs were not independently resolved.

Important corrections to supplied assets: Delhi's India Gate/Humayun's Tomb labels are swapped in the seeds. The Hawa Mahal URL returns 404. Supplied Varanasi URL is a person's portrait, Amritsar is Taj Mahal, and Udaipur is a South Indian temple; those mismatches have been replaced. Manali's snowy trail is visually plausible Himalayan scenery from the supplied package, but the exact trail location is unconfirmed.
